# AFROMATIONS Artist Covenant

## Purpose

AFROMATIONS exists to help independent tattoo artists, anime artists, manga creators, illustrators, and musicians build durable creative businesses without surrendering control of their work.

Our operating principle is simple:

> AI may accelerate the process. Human artists remain visible, paid, credited, and in control.

This covenant governs every AFROMATIONS artist relationship, Hana workflow, character launch, merchandise collaboration, and provenance record.

## The ten promises

1. **No training without explicit permission.** Artist work is not used to train a model unless the artist separately agrees in writing to the exact purpose, material, duration, and permitted outputs.
2. **No hidden ownership transfer.** Joining AFROMATIONS does not transfer ownership of pre-existing art, characters, flash, music, voice, likeness, or style.
3. **Every use requires a clear license.** Each approved use states the media, products, territory, duration, exclusivity, payment, approval rights, and termination terms.
4. **Human contributors are paid and credited.** AI mockups may guide production, but commissioned human finishing, drawing, tattoo design, animation, music, and editing are paid work.
5. **AI involvement is disclosed.** Each finished asset records what the artist made, what Hana or another system assisted with, and what the human artist changed or approved.
6. **Artists receive source files and records.** Final exports, approved source files, contracts, contribution records, and provenance evidence remain available to the artist.
7. **No publishing or sales without approval.** Hana may prepare, schedule, and recommend. The owner approves publishing, licensing, product launches, purchases, and destructive account actions.
8. **Revenue terms are visible before work begins.** Fees, royalties, commissions, production costs, platform costs, and payment timing are disclosed before acceptance.
9. **The artist is named in the record.** Public verification pages identify the original artist and paid collaborators unless a contributor requests a permitted private attribution arrangement.
10. **Artists can leave without losing their work.** Departure does not erase lawful ownership, source files, payment records, or completed provenance records.

## Artist payment floor

For AFROMATIONS-owned collaborative drops, the contributing artist receives **at least 40% of net profit**. This is a floor, not a ceiling.

Direct commissions and human finishing work are paid separately as labor. A project may therefore include:

- an agreed commission or finishing fee;
- reimbursement of approved production costs;
- a minimum 40% share of net profit on qualifying AFROMATIONS-owned drops;
- additional royalties or licensing payments where stated in the project agreement.

No artist is asked to work only for exposure.

## License types

AFROMATIONS separates ownership, authentication, and permission to use.

### 1. Tattoo license

For a specific tattoo use. It defines whether the design is exclusive or repeatable, whether alterations are allowed, who may apply it, and whether photographs may be used in promotion.

### 2. Character collaboration license

For defined use of an original character in story, animation, marketing, interactive media, or a Hana-powered campaign. The artist keeps ownership unless a separate signed assignment explicitly says otherwise.

### 3. Merchandise license

For defined products, channels, territory, term, approvals, royalty calculations, and inventory rules. Print-on-demand is preferred for initial drops to reduce waste and financial risk.

### 4. Training consent

A separate opt-in agreement for model training or fine-tuning. It is never bundled silently into another license.

## Authentication and provenance

Each approved work may receive an AFROMATIONS Sovereignty Record containing:

- certificate ID;
- creator and collaborator identities;
- artwork preview;
- source-file fingerprints;
- human contribution statement;
- AI tool and model disclosure;
- version history;
- license summary;
- payment and royalty references;
- trusted timestamp status;
- C2PA or compatible content-credential status where supported;
- optional blockchain batch anchor;
- public verification URL.

The certificate of authenticity and the license record are linked but remain distinct. The certificate documents the work and process. The license defines permission to use it.

## Hana's role

Hana is the operating agent behind the artist service. Hana may:

- organize artist intake and canon;
- create controlled mockups through Fal and other approved tools;
- prepare merchandise and campaign concepts;
- route work to paid human artists;
- manage approvals and contribution records;
- prepare content and promotion plans;
- monitor deadlines, costs, and royalty obligations;
- generate provenance manifests and verification records.

Hana may not silently publish, sign contracts, transfer rights, spend money, train on artist work, or change payout terms.

## Public promise

> Every line has an origin. Every artist gets named. Every use gets permission.

AFROMATIONS is artist-first, AI-assisted, human-finished, creator-owned, and built for long-term independence.
