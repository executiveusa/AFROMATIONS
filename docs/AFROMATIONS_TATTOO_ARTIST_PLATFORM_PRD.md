# AFROMATIONS Tattoo Artist Platform PRD

## Product decision

AFROMATIONS begins as a **local-first artist connection and promotion platform** for independent tattoo artists and anime/manga creators.

Dual is the flagship story and proof-of-work character. Hana is the repeatable operating product. AFROMATIONS connects artists to paid commissions, character collaborations, merchandise, licensing opportunities, promotion, and verifiable provenance.

The platform does not replace artists with AI. Hana uses AI for mockups, research, repetitive production, asset preparation, routing, and administration. Real artists perform approved human finishing and original creative work.

## Two-sided business model

### Artist side: Founding Artist Circle

Artists join a curated local roster at no upfront listing fee during the founding pilot.

AFROMATIONS provides:

- professional artist profile;
- portfolio and character presentation;
- promotion through AFROMATIONS channels;
- commission and collaboration leads;
- licensing templates and plain-language deal summaries;
- Hana-assisted mockups and campaign concepts;
- paid human-finishing opportunities;
- merchandise and digital-asset packaging;
- contribution and payment records;
- provenance certificates and verification pages;
- optional future Hana installation upsell.

### Customer side: Hana Character Launch Agent

Locked founding offer:

- **$1,495 paid in full**;
- **3 payments of $550 ($1,650 total)**;
- **optional maintenance: $249/month**.

The service turns one original character into a private AI-assisted creative studio with consistent assets, merchandise concepts, animation tests, promotion, provenance, voice/Telegram control, and paid human finishing.

## Artist economics

### AFROMATIONS-owned drops

The artist receives **at least 40% of net profit**. This is the minimum floor.

### Direct commissions

Human artists receive a separately stated labor fee. Platform commission, payment processing, approved production expenses, and any royalty share are disclosed before acceptance.

### Managed licensing and merchandise

Each project states:

- gross sale price;
- production and fulfillment costs;
- payment processing costs;
- AFROMATIONS service commission;
- artist royalty or profit share;
- payment schedule;
- return and refund treatment;
- approval and termination rules.

No artist is paid only in exposure.

## Rights model

Joining the platform grants no blanket ownership or training rights.

Every project selects one or more explicit permission records:

1. **Tattoo license** — one tattoo, limited edition, or repeatable flash use.
2. **Character collaboration license** — story, animation, campaign, or interactive use.
3. **Merchandise license** — named products, territory, channels, duration, and royalty terms.
4. **Promotional license** — permission to display work and artist attribution in AFROMATIONS marketing.
5. **Training consent** — separate optional permission for model training or fine-tuning.

A certificate of authenticity documents the work and process. A license record documents permission to use it. They must not be presented as the same thing.

## Certificate v0

Each certificate record must include:

- certificate ID;
- artwork title;
- creator name and profile;
- collaborators and roles;
- creation and approval dates;
- artwork preview;
- source-file SHA-256 fingerprints;
- version relationships;
- human contribution statement;
- AI assistance disclosure;
- license type and plain-language scope;
- payout or royalty reference;
- timestamp status;
- optional C2PA status;
- optional blockchain batch-anchor transaction;
- verification URL;
- revocation or correction history.

Do not place private source files, contracts, addresses, or unreleased media on a public blockchain.
