# AFROMATIONS Product Lock

## What AFROMATIONS Is

A **for-profit artist infrastructure company** with a social-purpose commitment.

Target users: **21+ serious artists and creative professionals.**

## Primary Product: Hana Artist Partner Program

**What it is:** An invite-only, paid program that installs a private AI studio agent (Hana) for an artist's creative business.

**What Hana does for a partner artist:**
1. Researches their niche and competition
2. Generates content (blogs, scripts, social packs)
3. Manages a publishing queue with human approval gates
4. Tracks provenance and authorship evidence
5. Builds and manages their artist landing page
6. Coordinates drops, auctions, and commission flows
7. Handles agent maintenance and content cadence

## What AFROMATIONS Is NOT

- Not a generic AI image generator
- Not a kids app or school platform
- Not a nonprofit
- Not an NFT marketplace
- Not a free tool
- Not a guaranteed IP protection service
- Not a law firm

## Social Purpose Commitment

AFROMATIONS operates as a for-profit company with a social-purpose commitment:
- Helping underrepresented and independent artists build technical fluency
- Protecting original creative work through provenance trails
- Creating sustainable creative businesses from original characters

This is not the primary marketing message. It is a structural commitment embedded in operations.

## 21+ Positioning

All public-facing language must reflect invite-only, 21+ professional positioning:

> "AfroMations is currently invite-only for 21+ artists and creative professionals."

Youth and community programs are future social-purpose tracks, not first-revenue products.
