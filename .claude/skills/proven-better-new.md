# Proven Better New

## Prime rule
Research the market before recommending product or landing-page direction. A founder claim is a hypothesis to test, not evidence.

## Workflow
1. Extract the underlying human instinct in one sentence without naming the product.
2. Run an analog hunt across direct competitors and adjacent products.
3. Pick the primary analog by audience match, not fame or revenue.
4. Autopsy the primary analog across acquisition, first-run, core loop, retention, monetization, distribution, and content/data moat.
5. Check failed or abandoned attempts serving the same instinct.
6. Classify mechanics as PROVEN, BETTER, or NEW.

## PROVEN
Use researched mechanics that already work for the same audience. Do not reinvent them without a documented reason.

## BETTER
A claimed improvement must be meaningful enough that current analog users would strongly prefer it. Smaller friction reductions often beat large speculative features.

## NEW
Treat unproven mechanics as experiments. Quarantine them so the company still works if they fail.

## AFROMATIONS application
For studio / community / agent work, research both:
- anime and animation studios;
- AI anime products;
- artist marketplaces and creative networks;
- social-purpose/community art organizations;
- creator-commerce and merchandise systems.

Always build a reference ledger with source, audience, mechanic, useful principle, what not to copy, and intended AFROMATIONS application.

## Output
Return evidence-backed Proven / Better / New considerations, the strongest tensions, and the smallest test that would resolve each unproven assumption. Do not fabricate traction or partnerships.
