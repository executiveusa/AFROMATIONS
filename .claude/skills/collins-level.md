# Collins-Level Governance

The standard is not modern, clean, premium, visually impressive, or technically deployed.

The standard is a clear, ownable, culturally relevant, emotionally compelling, commercially useful, accessible, responsive, technically verified experience.

## Order of operations
customer → problem → commercial/social outcome → positioning → content → proof → constraints → governing creative idea → design

## Required qualities
- recognizable brand expression
- editorial and visual craft
- Steve Krug-level usability
- real content and proof
- mobile designed separately
- accessible interaction
- independent verification
- owner control and rollback

## Reject
Generic AI aesthetics, centered-template heroes, default card walls, decorative gradients, fake metrics, unrelated stock imagery, meaningless motion, compressed-desktop mobile, and any design that could belong to fifty unrelated startups.

Do not imitate COLLINS. Imitate the rigor: strategic clarity, cultural intelligence, distinctive concepts, exceptional craft, system coherence, and measurable outcomes.
